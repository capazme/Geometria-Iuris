If you are landing here cold, read this file top to bottom; everything else is reached from here.

## 1. What the experiments do

Two experiments per Chapter 3, with three encoding conditions on three term pools.

| Pool | n | source | encoding | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **core** | 364 | curated post-BLP, K≥4 in HK Cap. enacted post-1989 | bare + attested | the headline pool |
| **bg** | 9.045 | residual legalish terms, K variable | bare + attested | robustness extensions |
| **control** | 100 | everyday vocabulary (pronouns, deixis, common nouns) | bare only | discriminative validity |

Two **lenses** consume these pools:

- **Lens I** (`experiment_1_structure/`) — Relational Distance Structure (§3.1). RDMs + Mantel + block bootstrap, with Holm correction across 17 pre-registered model pairs.
- **Lens IV** (`experiment_2_axes/`) — Value-axis projection (§3.2). 6 Kozlowski-style antonym axes built on the pool, projected per term, alignment across 45 model pairs.

Nine **extensions** (`ext/`) stress, qualify or operationalize the headline at term level. See §4 below.

Ten **language models** (`embeddings/`), grouped:
- WEIRD-side: BGE-EN-large, E5-large, FreeLaw-EN
- Sinic-side: BGE-ZH-large, Text2vec-large-ZH, Dmeta-ZH
- Bilingual control (same encoder, two languages): BGE-M3 (EN/ZH), Qwen3-0.6B (EN/ZH)

## 4. The nine extensions at a glance

| Ext | Role           | One-sentence finding |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | resource       | 9.045 bg routed to 7 domains via k=7 NN; mean confidence 0.515. CSV reusable for future pool expansions. |
| **D** | robustness ↑   | Δρ_sym attested = 0.538 → 0.590 as bg replaces core 0% → 75%. The tradition gap is **dominant** in encoded geometry, not curation-dependent. |
| **E** | generalization | 6 axes project on bg coherently per assigned domain. Pool-sensitive axes remain pool-sensitive even out-of-sample. |
| **F** | qualifier      | Low-confidence bg injected → Δρ_sym +0.027; high-confidence bg → -0.007. *Interpretive hint, n=20*. |
| **G** | term-level proof | Cross-encoder cosine **negative** (-0.05 to -0.11) but bilingual-control cosine **+0.5 to +0.75** on ~50 same-lemma bg. The divergence is in the tradition pair, not the encoder. |
| **H** | threshold      | ρ_cross attested monotonic with K: -0.13 (K=1) → +0.22 (K=8). Saturation around K≥4 — empirical justification of the pre-registered threshold. |
| **X** | dual of D      | Δρ_sym bare = 0.246 → 0.222 as control replaces core 0% → 27%. Direction correct (signal falls), effect small. |
| **Y** | **caveat**     | Δρ_sym on **100 control bare** = 0.156. Δρ_sym on **364 core bare** = 0.165. The bare signal is **encoder-tradition**, not legal-tradition. **The legal-tradition signal lives in the gap attested − bare** (0.543 − 0.165 = 0.378 on core; 0 − 0.156 = -0.156 on control). |
| **Z** | hierarchy fail | Only 3/10 models satisfy median(core×core) < median(core×bg) < median(core×control). In 7/10 the bg are *farther* from core than control are. Tier classification is corpus-curative, not geometric. |

## 5. The three results that anchor Chapter 4

1. **D + run-#3 stability**: Δρ_sym attested across two independently-curated pools and five mix levels is **structurally stable** at 0.541-0.590. → §4.1 opening.

2. **G + bilingual control**: same-lemma terms have **negative** cosine across the WEIRD×Sinic encoder pair but **+0.7** cosine on a single bilingual encoder. The cross-tradition divergence is *tradition-shaped*, not encoder-shaped. → §4.1 mid.

3. **Y caveat**: the bare signal (0.165) is indistinguishable from the same signal on everyday vocabulary (0.156). **The legal-meaning signal is the gap attested-bare, not the attested absolute value.** This reformulates the headline: Δρ_sym attested = 0.543 by itself is not *legal* signal; Δ(attested-bare) = 0.378 is. → §4.2 honest framing.

H, X, A are corroborative; E, F, Z are qualifying caveats for §4.2.
